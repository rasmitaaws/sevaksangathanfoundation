OUR FLAGSHIP VISION

A safe and sustainable Goshala

A community shelter for rescued, abandoned, stray and vulnerable cattle in Sauria, Puri.

🐄 🐄 🐄 🐄 🐄
SAURIA • PURI • ODISHAFrom a vision to a place of care

6+care priorities
100%community spirit

More than a shelter — a place of dignity.

The planned goshala is envisioned with shelter, water, fodder, veterinary support, drainage and responsible waste management. The aim is to combine animal welfare with community participation and sustainable practices.

  • Rescue and rehabilitation of vulnerable cattle
  • Clean shelter, food and reliable water
  • Veterinary care and recovery support
  • Fodder, composting and sustainability initiatives
  • Volunteer and local employment opportunities
